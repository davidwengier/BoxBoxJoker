How to play

Box Box Jokers runs automatically while the page is open. Build up cash and reputation at your tables, open packs to find Jokers, and equip those Jokers to make every hand more valuable.

Quick start

  1. Open the free Opening Night Pack on the Card Packs page.
  2. Open a casino table and use Manage Jokers to equip what you found.
  3. Let guests play automatically to earn cash and reputation.
  4. Buy more packs, unlock tables, and upgrade table speed as you grow.

Casino tables

  • Each unlocked table automatically deals a five-card poker hand when its timer reaches zero.
  • Better hands pay more and award more reputation. Every hand also earns the table's base fee.
  • New tables require both the displayed cash and reputation, and later tables have more Joker slots.
  • Speed upgrades shorten a table's timer, down to one second per hand.
  • Use the floor and table-set buttons to move between all 27 tables.

Goals and achievements

  • The Goals page tracks milestones for guests, collection progress, tables, Joker levels, cash, and reputation.
  • A badge appears when rewards are ready, and completed goals move to the front.
  • Claimed goals become achievements and remain recorded in your save.
  • Goal rewards are cash only; they do not award luck or exclusive Jokers.

Earnings dashboard

  • The Dashboard shows rolling casino income per second and lifetime cash earned.
  • See total table income, your strongest owned Joker, and the hand that has earned the most cash.
  • Each unlocked table records its total hands, lifetime earnings, and average income per hand.
  • Dashboard history is saved automatically and continues building as your casino plays.

Jokers and levels

  • Equipped Jokers add cash, multiply income, reward certain cards or hands, or improve the chance of special hands.
  • A Joker can only be equipped at one table at a time, and a table cannot equip the same Joker twice.
  • Finding a Joker you already own upgrades its first copy instead of adding another copy.
  • Each extra Joker level increases its cash bonus, multiplier, or special-hand chance by 25%.

Card packs

  • Each pack contains ordinary cards and Jokers from its displayed pool. The free pack can only be opened once.
  • Use View contents to see every possible Joker and the average odds for each rarity.
  • Use Buy 5 to open five paid packs together. The same Joker cannot appear twice inside one pack.
  • Themed packs contain exclusive Jokers, while free-pack Jokers can also appear in paid packs.

Luck and auto-sell

  • Luck upgrades improve the rarity weights for Epic, Legendary, and Mythic Jokers.
  • Turn on Auto-sell and select rarities you want converted directly into cash.
  • Auto-sell applies to the first card too. A sold card is still marked as discovered in your collection.
  • Ordinary, Common, Uncommon, Rare, and Limited odds are not directly boosted by luck.

Collection

  • The Collection page tracks every pack Joker and all 52 ordinary playing cards.
  • Ordinary cards do not provide bonuses, but they count toward collection completion.
  • Filter Jokers by name, ownership, rarity, pack, or effect. Owned results always appear first.
  • Each Joker shows its effect, level, and the packs where it can be found after discovery.
  • Complete every ordinary card and pack Joker to unlock the secret collection reward.

The Dug and secret shop

  • Completing the collection unlocks The Dug, a cash reward, and the hidden Dug Shop.
  • Equip The Dug to improve Flush luck, multiply table income, and earn one Dug Coin from each Flush.
  • Spend Dug Coins on permanent bonuses for poker hands or improve the chance of a special Dug customer.
  • When The Dug visits as a customer, your entire current cash balance is doubled.

Saving and resetting

  • Your casino saves automatically in this browser after purchases, upgrades, equipment changes, and completed hands.
  • Progress belongs to this browser and device, so clearing browser storage also clears the save.
  • Reset game permanently deletes the local save after you confirm and starts again from $300.
